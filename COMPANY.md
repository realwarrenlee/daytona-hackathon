## 13. Sustainability and green cleaning

- Uses Singapore Green Label certified cleaning chemicals where available, and dispenses them through dilution-control units.
- Microfibre cloths and mops, colour-coded, to cut water and chemical use.
- Battery-powered equipment; diesel equipment phased out in 2024.
- Recycling and waste segregation at all sites; monthly waste data provided to clients on request.
- Water use tracked against site baselines under the ISO 14001 system.
- Target (fictional): cut chemical use per square metre by 15% by FY2027/28 against FY2024/25.

## 14. Quality and complaints handling

- **Inspections:** Site supervisors inspect daily; Area Supervisors do weekly joint inspections, and management does monthly audits against the ISO 9001 checklist.
- **Performance measures:** Inspection score (target at least 90%), complaint response time and closure time.
- **Complaints:** Logged through the QR system, phone or email. Staff acknowledge within 15 minutes during operating hours and fix within 2 hours for routine issues. Repeated complaints go to the Operations Manager for root-cause review.
- **Client reviews:** Monthly reports and quarterly review meetings with each client.
- **Results (fictional, FY2025/26):** Average inspection score 93%; 96% of complaints closed within target.

## 16. Bid policy and preferences

- **Target contract size:** S$0.5M to S$10M total value, 2 to 3 years with option years.
- **Preferred sectors:** Education campuses, statutory board offices, community and healthcare buildings, town council conservancy.
- **Regions:** Island-wide, with a preference for the east and north-east, close to the Kaki Bukit depot.
- **Avoids:**
  - M&E engineering, building services maintenance and any tender requiring BCA ME workheads
  - Tenders requiring BCA FM02 L6 or contract values above the L5 limit
  - Integrated FM (FM01) as main contractor
  - Hospital wards and clinical areas needing specialist infection-control cleaning
  - Marine, industrial plant and hazardous-waste cleaning
- **Pricing approach:** Labour costs built from PWM wage levels; no below-PWM pricing. Typical margin target is 4% to 6%.
- **Partnering:** Will act as a cleaning subcontractor to integrated FM firms where the main tender needs workheads it does not hold.
- **Go/no-go owner:** Contracts and Tender Manager, with sign-off by the Managing Director.
