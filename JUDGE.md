Judging Criteria & Rules

AI-POWERED SHORTLISTING IN EFFECT
Submissions are ingested and scored by AI evaluation agents to shortlist top finalist teams for live stage demos,

1. Completeness
Did the team ship at least a minimum viable product (MVP)? Working software aver slides.

2. Innovation
Is the product idea novel? Moving past simple prompt wrappers toward autonomous feedback loops.

3. Real-Life Problem
Is the project solving a genuine, real-life problem or verified market friction point?

4. Sponsor Tech
Did the team meaningfully integrate Daytona sandboxes or Nosana decentralized compute?
